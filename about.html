About the lab

Dr. Hira Benish, a mathematician specializing in graph theory, founded Math & AI Lab as an independent research and innovation initiative.

Our vision

We want mathematicians to help shape the future of AI. That means looking beyond familiar applications, asking questions that today’s models leave open, and finding new roles for mathematical insight in research, education, and industry.

Our mission

We help mathematicians take a question from theory to a working experiment. Through computational tools, visual lessons, and collaborative research, we explore where skills such as proof, modeling, and graph analysis can meet emerging needs in AI.

A mathematical idea takes on a new life when someone can experiment with it.
