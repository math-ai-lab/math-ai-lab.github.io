About the lab

Math & AI Lab is an independent research and innovation initiative founded by Dr. Hira Benish, a mathematician specializing in graph theory.

Our vision

We want students, educators, and researchers to understand the mathematics behind intelligent systems well enough to question how they work and imagine what they could do differently.

Our mission

We turn mathematical questions into experiments people can run. Through interactive tools, research prototypes, and visual lessons, we connect graph theory and data science with the practice of building and testing AI.
