Educational technology · In development

ThinkXP

An interactive learning and assessment application that brings questions, gameplay, and learner progress into one experience.

The learning problem

A single score gives learners little sense of the questions they have worked through. ThinkXP brings assessment activities and progress into the same learning experience.

How it works

Work through a question, submit a response, and continue through the activity. Learner progress provides a record across the experience.

Educational foundations

Question-based practice gives learners repeated opportunities to retrieve and apply knowledge. An evaluation of ThinkXP’s learning outcomes remains future work.

Development & credits

Developed by Dr. Hira Benish. ThinkXP is undergoing deployment preparation. Public access, technical documentation, and release details are pending verification.
